## 1. Design Read
Reading this as: Modern Clean Engineering Portfolio for Technical Recruiters and Engineering Managers, in a Crisp Light Technical style with Slate & Teal accents, dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

---

## 2. Visual Identity & Dials
- **ENERGY (2 - Balanced):** Clean, crisp, focused engineering aesthetic on bright slate-50/white surfaces with deep teal accents (`#0e7490` / `#0891b2`).
- **RHYTHM (2 - Structured & Varied):** Varied layouts per section (Hero with interactive pipeline architecture card, Selected Projects with card overlays and contribution highlights, About & Education with stats and high-education card, Engineering Skill Matrix, and structured timeline for Work Experience).
- **MOTION (1 - Purposeful & Subtle):** Subtle transitions and hover feedback on cards, buttons, and links. No distracting continuous floating animations.

---

## 3. Color Palette & Hierarchy
- **Primary Background:** Slate 50 (`#f8fafc`) for clean, airy technical readability.
- **Card & Surface Background:** Pure White (`#ffffff`) with subtle 1px border in Slate 200 (`#e2e8f0`).
- **Primary Brand Accent:** Deep Teal (`#0e7490` / `#0f766e`) for main buttons, section titles, and active indicators.
- **Pill Badge Accents:** Soft Cyan (`#e0f2fe`) and Soft Purple (`#f3e8ff`) with high-contrast text.
- **Text Hierarchy:**
  - Headings / Important: Slate 900 (`#0f172a`).
  - Subheadings / Emphasized: Deep Teal 800 (`#115e59` / `#155e75`).
  - Body Text: Slate 700 (`#334155`), ensuring minimum 5:1 contrast against white/slate-50.
  - Secondary/Meta Labels: Slate 500 (`#64748b`).

---

## 4. Typography
- **Primary Sans:** Inter / Geist Sans for high readability in dense technical descriptions.
- **Monospace Code/Data:** JetBrains Mono / Geist Mono for Go code snippets, pipeline steps, and tech tags.

---

## 5. Architectural Principles (Anti-Slop Alignment)
- **Zero Em Dashes in Copy:** Comma, colon, parentheses, or periods only.
- **Real Evidence Over AI Claims:** Real GPA 3.65, real internships at BRI and Indocement, real projects.
- **Every Interactive Element Works:** All buttons, filters, links, and CV download trigger real accessible destinations.
- **Accessible & Responsive:** WCAG AA contrast compliance, keyboard navigable (`Tab`, visible focus rings), flawless layout on mobile (320px+).
